In brief

Idea-stage teams are welcome.

Five stages, from IDEA to GROWTH, are recorded separately from incorporation. An unincorporated project is labeled exactly that.

Every unit comes from the startup.

No order book, no resale, no leverage. Each purchase is new issuance at the founder’s fixed price, and the proceeds land in the round account.

The estimate shows its work.

Inputs, weights, model version and a sensitivity band are printed next to every suggested price. The founder can override it, with a reason on the record.

How a share is born in five dispatches

Founder journey · about 15 minutes
No. 1

Profile

Write the story, not a deck. Stage, sector, founders, website and LinkedIn links. You choose which numbers are public and which stay private.

→ Public with consent
No. 2

Estimate

An open, versioned formula scores product, traction, team and financial readiness against your stage, then shows every assumption and a sensitivity band.

→ valuation-model-v1
No. 3

Price

Accept the anchor or override it with a reason. Set a fixed unit price and a finite cap on new units. Published terms never quietly change.

→ Immutable terms
No. 4

Link

Paste one “Invest in us” link on your site, your bio, your demo-day slide. Anyone who believes early follows it straight to your round.

→ One URL
No. 5

Settle

Each purchase debits the backer, credits your round and issues units in one atomic transaction. No double charges. No oversold rounds.

Clipping · The formula, in full
# valuation-model-v1 · demo anchors, not market data
score      = 0.30·product + 0.30·traction
           + 0.25·team    + 0.15·financial
multiplier = 0.50 + score / 100
anchor     = stage_anchor × multiplier
band       = [anchor × 0.60, anchor × 1.50]
unit_price = anchor / founder_units

Unknown answers score zero and lower evidence quality. That isn’t a verdict on your company; it just means we haven’t seen the evidence yet. A higher claim never creates credits or verification.

Corrections & Clarifications

Most pitches overstate something on page one. Here’s what this one doesn’t claim.

Units issued on Prospecto today are simulated. They’re bought with free demo credits and carry no legal ownership, cash value, redemption or dividends.

The estimate is a transparent heuristic with invented stage anchors, not an appraisal or investment advice.

Followers aren’t investors, and popularity isn’t value. Real equity is a future integration, and virtual units never convert automatically.

Late edition · how to read a round

One company. One fixed price. Three columns.

Column one

Who already backed it.

Every purchase is printed the moment it settles: time, units, total. No bids, no asks, just people who believed early.

Column two

One company, one fixed price, one honest chart.

The price doesn’t move. The line that rises is how much of the round has been issued.

The founders’ story, team and dated evidence sit right underneath.

“Nobody bids. Nobody asks. You either believe early, or you watch.”
Column three

Pick a quantity. Get an exact quote.

The quote shows unit price, fee and your allocation at full subscription. Then you confirm once.
